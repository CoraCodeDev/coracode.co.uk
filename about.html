About CoraCode

A small software studio building products, developer tools, and open source experiments.

📱

Products

End-user apps and services. Things people actually use — like PDF Splitter, a free desktop app for Windows. Built to be useful, not fancy.

⚙️

Developer Tools

Tools that make our workflow easier — and hopefully yours too. Open source things like our OpenClaw skills: Joplin notes integration, Tado smart thermostat control, and more. Built in the open because we believe tools should be transparent and modifiable.

🔬

Experiments

Quick builds and proofs of concept. Not everything becomes a product, but everything teaches us something. The best ideas start as "what if we tried this?"

Why We Started

We kept building things that solved our own problems, then realising other people had the same problems. So we started publishing them.

CoraCode is the banner we put on all of that. A home for the things we build, a place where people can find them, and a way to connect the dots between projects.

Our Philosophy

We believe in building things that work, documenting them properly, and shipping them when they're ready — not when they're perfect. We prefer small, focused tools over big platforms. We like open source because it makes better software and better communities.

We also believe that the best way to learn is to build in public. So we'll share what we're working on, what breaks, and what we learn along the way.
